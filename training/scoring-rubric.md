# TRACE Scoring Rubric — 100 points

| Domain | Points | Full-credit requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Integrity & provenance | 10 | Hashes/provenance checked before conclusions |
| Timeline | 15 | UTC normalization and defensible event order |
| Identity/authentication | 10 | Account activity correctly contextualized |
| Execution | 10 | Execution finding supported by artifacts |
| Cross-artifact correlation | 15 | Important finding independently corroborated |
| Movement/persistence/evasion | 15 | Applicable mechanisms correctly evidenced; N/A used when unsupported |
| Attack reconstruction | 15 | Coherent evidence-supported sequence |
| Reporting | 10 | Exact artifacts cited, uncertainty/confidence stated |

## Result bands
- **90–100:** Senior-ready
- **80–89:** Strong investigation
- **70–79:** Competent, correlation gaps remain
- **Below 70:** Re-analysis required

A correct guess without supporting evidence does not receive full credit.
